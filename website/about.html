About

The oracle
as magazine.

"Every AI style app is a wardrobe mirror. Outfit Oracle is not a mirror."

Every other style app in this category starts with the same assumption: you want to see your clothes reflected back at you. Upload your wardrobe. Scroll through items you already own. The app organises. The app suggests combinations. The app is, fundamentally, passive.

Outfit Oracle starts from a different premise. The weather already decided something today. The Oracle is just translating it.

You enter a city. You choose an occasion. The Oracle fetches the live weather — temperature, humidity, wind, UV index, precipitation — and asks Claude to deliver a verdict. Not a suggestion. A verdict. What to wear, what to avoid, and why. Structured outfit picks with editorial reasoning, calibrated to your aesthetic profile and budget tier.

It's a publication, not a wardrobe manager. An authority speaking to you, not a catalog waiting to be browsed.

The design

Outfit Oracle ships with 13 visual themes — from the warm cream editorial authority of Classic to the de Stijl grid of Mondrian, the bubbly fashion-club energy of Y2K, and the TREVO-inspired Electric. Each theme is a complete visual world: its own typography, colour system, motion spec, and layout grammar.

The core aesthetic — across Classic and the Editorial themes — is Cormorant Garamond for headlines, IBM Plex Mono or Space Mono for data labels, cream or near-black surfaces, and a single scarlet element per screen. Sharp corners everywhere. No rounded cards. No decorative blobs. Typography is the decoration.

The design principle, borrowed from fashion publishing: cold information rendered with the heat of obsession. The app should feel like it cost something to make. Not delight — recognition. Like opening a magazine you didn't know you'd been waiting for.

The technology

Outfit Oracle is an iOS app built with React Native (Expo bare workflow). Weather data comes from Open-Meteo — a free, open-source API with no account required. Outfit recommendations are generated by Claude Sonnet 4.6 via a Cloudflare Worker proxy that keeps the API key server-side and out of the app bundle.

There is no wardrobe database. There is no social graph. There is no cloud storage of your preferences beyond the style profile you set on your own device. The Oracle's job is to give you a verdict for today, not to accumulate data about you.
