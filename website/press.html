About

Outfit Oracle is a daily AI fashion advisor that tells you what to wear based on the weather you're actually in. Unlike every other style app — which asks you to catalog your wardrobe and see your own clothes reflected back — Outfit Oracle is an authority that speaks to you. Enter a city. Choose an occasion: Work, Date, Weekend, Event, or Active. Receive a structured verdict: outfit picks, what to avoid, and an editorial read on the day's vibe. The app uses live Open-Meteo weather data and Claude Sonnet AI to generate recommendations calibrated to your aesthetic profile, budget tier, and personal colour preferences. It ships with 13 visual themes, 127+ achievements, and three personality modes — Diplomatic, Editorial, and Savage.
