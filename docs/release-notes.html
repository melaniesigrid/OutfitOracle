Release Notes

Site and app updates are tracked here in reverse chronological order, grouped by month.

May 2026

  1. 14 May 2026

    Y2K theme, confetti, and temperature controls

    Outfit Oracle 1.3.0 adds a full Y2K design system with lavender, hot pink, lime accents, dedicated Y2K components, and two font subthemes. Badge unlocks now feel more celebratory with confetti and a springing toast animation.

    The release also introduces an hourly temperature graph and a Celsius/Fahrenheit setting that carries through weather strips, archive entries, and forecasts.

  2. 14 May 2026

    Theme system and achievement toasts

    Outfit Oracle 1.2.0 introduces Classic, Editorial Light, and Editorial Dark themes, with theme selection saved across sessions. Achievement badge toasts now appear when users unlock weather badges, and cold-start badge spam has been fixed.

    This update also makes status bars theme-aware, improves editorial error copy, and documents the design system for future contributors.

  3. 14 May 2026

    Reliability fixes for launch and badges

    Outfit Oracle 1.1.1 fixes several persistence and startup edge cases, including founding member badge storage, storage read failures, and font load failures. These changes prevent the splash screen from hanging if local storage or font loading rejects.

    The release also cleans up locked achievement presentation and expands the automated test suite around onboarding and badge behavior.

  4. 13 May 2026

    Founding member badge and onboarding gate

    Outfit Oracle 1.1.0 adds the founding member badge for the first 100 unique devices, with server-controlled eligibility through Cloudflare KV. Style onboarding is now required before entering the app, replacing the previous optional skip path.

    The worker also gains stricter device identifier validation and hybrid rate limiting, while related memoization and TypeScript issues are cleaned up.

  5. 12 May 2026

    Initial launch

    Outfit Oracle 1.0.0 launches with weather-powered outfit recommendations, a Cloudflare Worker AI proxy, and an editorial cream, black, and scarlet interface. The first build includes Today, Oracle, and You tabs with forecasts, outfit history, saved looks, and sharing.

    The launch also includes style profiles, consult streaks, achievements, weekly challenges, GPS city detection, privacy controls, Sentry crash reporting, and Open-Meteo weather data.
