# Vision Goals

## Ultimate Goal

Build Outfit Oracle into a recognisable, iconic fashion-tech app — one that fashion magazines, luxury brands, and major retailers want to acquire or partner with. The product should feel like it belongs in the same conversation as Vogue, Net-a-Porter, and the LVMH ecosystem: authoritative, beautiful, and indispensable.

## Goal Items

- [ ] Mass market downloads — enough scale to be a credible acquisition or partnership target
- [ ] Brand recognition in fashion-tech — Outfit Oracle is known by name in the space
- [ ] Partnership or licensing deal with a fashion magazine, brand, or retailer
- [ ] Acquisition or strategic investment from a fashion giant (LVMH, Condé Nast, Hearst, a major retailer)
- [ ] Monetisation proven — paid tier or brand partnership revenue demonstrated before exit
- [ ] App Store presence that signals credibility — ratings, reviews, editorial features
- [ ] Weather alerts for Canada and US — complete weather context for North American users
- [ ] Try-before-profile funnel — reduce TTHV to < 1 min, convert casual browsers to retained users
- [ ] Paid tier with RevenueCat — unlock unlimited consults and Pro image features
- [ ] Style Presets — curated occasion templates that improve verdict quality and reduce blank-page anxiety
