## North star

**Positioning:** The version of Vogue that knows your weather.

**Tagline:** Your unsolicited style authority.

**Category:** Daily AI fashion oracle (weather + occasion + taste) — **not** a wardrobe catalog app.

**Primary job:** “What should I wear today in this city, for this occasion?”

**Business outcome (2026 H1):** Install on real devices via TestFlight → external beta → App Store → grow an owned audience on Instagram before paid acquisition.

---

## Target audience

| Segment | Who | Why they care |
|---------|-----|----------------|
| **Core** | Style-literate adults 25–40, iOS, NA/UK | Follow fashion, hate decision fatigue, check weather daily |
| **Travel** | Frequent flyers / relocators | New city + unknown forecast = high consult intent |
| **Occasion** | Work, date, event planners | Need a verdict, not a mood board |
| **Aspirational** | Quiet luxury → contemporary budget | Profile budget tier maps to affiliate tiers later |

**Anti-audience (do not optimize messaging for):** Wardrobe-inventory power users (Indyx/Whering), one-off human stylist shoppers (Wishi), deal hunters.

---

## Marketing phases (aligned with launch)

| Phase | Timing | Goal | Primary channels |
|-------|--------|------|------------------|
| **A — Presence** | Now (pre–public launch) | Credibility for CJ, App Store, press; build waitlist habit | Instagram, website, email aliases |
| **B — Beta** | External TestFlight | 5–20 trusted testers; feedback + screenshots | Instagram DMs, Stories, TestFlight link in bio |
| **C — Launch** | App Store live | Downloads, first consult, reviews | ASO, Instagram, share cards in-app |
| **D — Grow** | Post-launch 30–90d | Retention, UGC, affiliate clicks | Instagram Reels, city features, optional micro-influencers |
| **E — Monetize** | D7 retention > 30% | CJ affiliate + later Oracle Pro | In-app shop rails, email to engaged users |

**Rule from Roadmap:** Ship growth features before heavy monetization. Marketing should sell the **oracle loop**, not subscriptions, until launch metrics justify Pro.

## Content pillars (rotate weekly)

1. **The Verdict** — Real city + weather + occasion + screenshot (core product demo).
2. **Oracle Voice** — Diplomatic vs Editorial vs Savage comparison (same city, different tone).
3. **Weather Drama** — Extreme cold, rain, heat; editorial copy from avoid list.
4. **City Passport** — Map / fashion capitals / “Oracle visited…”
5. **Theme Drop** — Y2K, Mondrian, Editorial Dark visual flex.
6. **Word of the Day** — Fashion vocabulary from TodayScreen list.
7. **Build in Public** — TestFlight, features shipped, design decisions (sparse — 1×/month max).
8. **Social Proof** — Testimonials, tester quotes, App Store rating (post-launch).

**Never lead with:** “AI-powered” as the headline. Lead with **weather + authority + outfit**.

## Brand voice (captions & Stories)

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Confident, dry wit | Startup hustle (“excited to announce!!!”) |
| Short sentences | Hashtag walls (max 12 tags) |
| Specific cities & temps | Vague “elevate your style” |
| “Verdict,” “edit,” “the Oracle” | “Utilize,” “leverage,” “game-changer” |
| Acknowledge commission when linking shop (later) | Hidden affiliate posts |

**Sample lines:**
- “Linen in April. The Oracle has notes.”
- “11°C, overcast, Work. Structure wins.”
- “You checked the forecast. You still panicked. Same.”
